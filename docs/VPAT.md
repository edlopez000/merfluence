# Accessibility Conformance Report

### Voluntary Product Accessibility Template® (VPAT®), Revised Section 508 Edition

**Version 2.5**

**Name of Product/Version:** Merfluence 1.2.0

**Report Date:** 1 August 2026

**Product Description:** A free, open-source Confluence Cloud macro, built on Atlassian
Forge, that renders [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/) diagrams from text. Diagrams are
rendered entirely in the reader's browser; the app has no backend, requests no API scopes,
and makes no network calls of its own. It provides two user interfaces: a **reader view**
that displays a rendered diagram with pan, zoom, fullscreen, and export controls, and an
**editor** for writing the diagram source.

**Contact Information:** support@edwardlopez.dev ·
[github.com/edlopez000/merfluence](https://github.com/edlopez000/merfluence)

**Notes:** This report covers only the two interfaces Merfluence itself ships. The
Confluence page, its editor, and the macro-insertion flow are Atlassian products and are
out of scope. The **content of a diagram** — its colours, labels, and structure — is
authored by the page author in Mermaid source; the app renders that content faithfully and
cannot correct it. Criteria affected by that boundary are marked _Partially Supports_ with
the reason stated. WCAG Level AAA is out of scope and its rows are omitted.
[ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md) is the authoritative prose statement and carries
the same findings; where the two differ, it governs.

**Evaluation Methods Used:** Maintainer self-assessment of the shipped source against the
WCAG 2.1 Level A and AA success criteria, supported by the project's automated test suite
(unit tests in jsdom plus a real-Chromium browser suite covering the render pipeline, the
computed accessible name of every diagram type, and each keyboard interaction). Names and
roles were verified by inspecting Chromium's accessibility tree. **No independent
third-party audit and no assistive-technology user testing have been performed.**

## Terms

- **Supports** — the functionality of the product has at least one method that meets the
  criterion without known defects, or meets it with equivalent facilitation.
- **Partially Supports** — some functionality of the product does not meet the criterion.
- **Does Not Support** — the majority of product functionality does not meet the criterion.
- **Not Applicable** — the criterion is not relevant to the product.
